# D3C.2S Doctrine-Leg Explanation Enrichment Audit

## Purpose

D3C.2S creates a read-only diagnostic endpoint that explains the doctrine legs behind behavioral-resolution evidence.

## Doctrine Legs

D3C.2S separates:

- Demand / support validation
- Supply exhaustion validation
- Contrary failure
- Structurally meaningful location
- SML quality
- SML locations
- Footprint archetypes
- Doctrine reason

## Guardrails

D3C.2S:

- does not write to Supabase
- does not mutate campaigns
- does not confirm operator control
- does not unconfirm operator control
- does not execute D3D
- does not use D3D as source
- does not affect score
- does not affect rank
- does not affect campaign state
- does not affect transitions
- does not affect gamma/options
- does not create trade signals

## No-Drift Statement

D3C.2S is explanatory evidence only. Complete doctrine-leg evidence remains unconfirmed unless D3D is separately authorized under its production mutation protocol.
